03

Fill out your application form, upload required documents, and submit before the deadline.

Our admissions team will review your application and notify you via email within 2–4 weeks.

Admission Requirements

  • O'Level result (minimum 5 credits including English & Mathematics)
  • Valid JAMB/UTME score (minimum 180)
  • Birth certificate or statutory declaration of age
  • Passport photograph (white background)
  • Letter of recommendation (optional but encouraged)
